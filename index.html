About

I am a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Michigan, advised by Prof. Gabor Orosz. My research focuses on vehicle dynamics and control, with particular interest in Model Predictive Control (MPC) and Control Barrier Function (CBF) frameworks.

Previously, I received a B.S. degree in Mechanical Engineering (summa cum laude) from Yonsei University in 2024, and completed my M.S. at Yonsei University in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Jun Young Yoon. My master's research centered on a 6-DOF magnetic levitation system with DNN-approximated MPC implemented on FPGA for 10 kHz real-time control.

My research interests include:

  • Vehicle dynamics and control
  • Model Predictive Control (MPC)
  • Control Barrier Functions (CBF)
  • Design and motion control of mechatronics systems

DNN-Approximated Offset-Free MPC on FPGA for 10-kHz Constrained Control of 6-DOF Magnetic Levitation System

Model predictive control (MPC) is well known for its high performance in motion control applications. However, due to the requirement of kHz-level sampling rates in 6-DOF magnetic levitation systems, MPC has rarely been applied to such systems. I develop an MPC structure lightweight enough for 10 kHz real-time control while achieving offset-free tracking performance, with a DNN approximating the QP solver and implemented on FPGA.

C.H. Kim, H.M. Yoon, J.W. Jeong, E.K. Kim and J.Y. Yoon, "DNN-Approximated Offset-Free MPC on FPGA for 10-kHz Constrained In-Plane Control of 6-DOF Magnetic Levitation System," IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics (under review).

under review IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics

Design and Control of a Novel 6-DOF Magnetic Levitation System

A novel 6-DOF magnetic levitation system combining reluctance and Lorentz forces to achieve both long-stroke actuation and zero-power gravity compensation. Unlike conventional Lorentz-only systems that require constant current, the proposed dual-mode design uses reluctance forces for passive gravity support and Lorentz forces for dynamic motion. A working prototype demonstrated stable levitation with only 0.498 W power consumption at rest.

C.H. Kim, H.M. Yoon, J.W. Jeong, E.K. Kim and J.Y. Yoon, "Design and Control of Dual Reluctance-Lorentz 6-DOF Magnetic Levitator for In-Plane Long-Stroke Motion and Low-Power Vertical Levitation," IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics (under review).

Conference PRESM 2025

Vehicle Lateral Dynamics Control

A gain-scheduled feedforward-feedback control system to reduce lateral acceleration, jerk, and tracking error during vehicle lane changes. The controller combines a feedforward module based on path geometry with an LQR feedback controller, using a 2-DOF dual-track vehicle model and a Luenberger-PI observer. Simulations at 60–120 kph showed up to 79.6% jerk reduction and 54.2% tracking error reduction compared to a feedback-only approach.

C. Kim (presenter), J. Kee, H. Jang, J. Wang and J. Yoon, "Gain-Scheduled Feedforward-Feedback Control for Reducing Lateral Acceleration and Jerk of Vehicle During Lane Changing Maneuver," PRESM 2025. [Abstract]

Intelligent Controller Development for PAPS-based Robotic C-arm

In this project, we are developing intelligent control methods for a robotic C-arm equipped with a PAPS (Pre-Auto Positioning System). Automated minimum-time and collision-free paths and trajectories for a 4-DOF robotic C-arm are required to minimize X-ray exposure and provide a user-friendly interface. To achieve this, we are developing sub-optimal collision-free paths and trajectories using A* and RRT algorithms. We are also implementing and testing these methods in a MATLAB simulation environment to ensure safe motion planning and strict avoidance of the patient area. This work aims to improve the operational safety and efficiency of next-generation robotic C-arm systems.

Image reference: Korea Patent 10-2010-0082406, Method for Operating X-ray Diagnosis Machine.

Adaptive Control for Payload-Invariant Performance in a 6-DOF Magnetic Levitation System

A control method to maintain dynamic performance of a 6-DOF hybrid-reluctance magnetic levitation system under changing payload masses. A mass compensator, designed as a modified lead compensator, counteracts the dynamics altered by the payload. Experimental results showed that the crossover frequency was restored from 30 Hz to 50 Hz, and RMS tracking error was reduced by 98% under a 5 Hz circular motion command.

C. Kim (presenter), H. Yoon, J. Jung and J. Yoon, "Control Method for Maintaining Dynamic Performance of a 6-DOF Hybrid-reluctance-type Magnetic Levitator During Payload Mass Changes," PRESM 2024. [Abstract]

Published Journal of KSPE · Collaboration with LIG Nex1 Co., Ltd.

Laser CBC SPGD Control Algorithm Modeling & Simulation

An adaptive SPGD (Stochastic Parallel Gradient Descent) algorithm to improve convergence speed and stability in coherent beam combining (CBC) systems. Unlike conventional SPGD with fixed control parameters, the proposed method dynamically adjusts perturbation amplitude and learning rate based on real-time feedback. Simulations on 7- and 19-channel CBC systems showed 26.4% and 18.1% reductions in iteration count, respectively.

H.M. Yoon, S.M. Lee, J.W. Jung, K. Lee, J.J. Jung, C.H. Kim and J.Y. Yoon, "Study on Characteristics of SPGD Algorithm in Coherent Beam Combining with Phase and Tip-tilt Control Speed Differences," Journal of the Korean Society for Precision Engineering. [PDF]

Published IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics

Force Ripple Reduction Control of a PMLSM

A control-based method to reduce force ripples in iron-cored permanent-magnet linear synchronous motors (PMLSMs). By adjusting DQ-axis currents, the method actively cancels both tangential and normal force ripples using a physics-based force model. The proposed method achieved 87% reduction in tangential and 69% in normal-direction ripple (RMS).

S.M. Lee, Y.S. Kwon, J.W. Jeong, K.J. Kang, C.H. Kim and J.Y. Yoon, "Identification and Reduction of Normal-Direction Force Ripples in Permanent-Magnet Linear Synchronous Motors," IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics. [PDF]

Under Review IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics

6-DOF Magnetic Levitation System for High-Throughput Optical Fiber Alignment

A compact 6-DOF magnetic levitation system combined with a real-time feedback-based Dynamic Spiral Searching (DSS) control algorithm for fully automated alignment of single-mode optical fibers. Unlike conventional multi-stage setups, this integrated system achieves long-range motion, fast dynamic response, and submicron precision within a single actuator platform.

H.M. Yoon, C.H. Kim, K.J. Kang, J.M. Sung, E.K. Kim … and J.Y. Yoon, "Active 6-DOF Magnetic Levitator for Fully-Automated High-Throughput Large-Range Single-Mode Optical Fiber Alignment," IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics (under review).
